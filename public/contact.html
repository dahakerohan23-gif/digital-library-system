Borrowing Duration

Standard borrowing limit is 7 days per title. Members can hold up to 3 books concurrently.

💰

Overdue Fine Policy

Fines are automatically calculated at a flat rate of ₹5.00 per day overdue. Fines can be settled with the library administrator.

🔖

Advance Booking

If all copies of a title are checked out, use Advance Booking to enter the priority reserve queue. The system holds the book when returned.

Sunday & Public Holidays: 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM
